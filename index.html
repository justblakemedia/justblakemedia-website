What happens after the first call

Start with the actual problem

  1. Diagnose

    Tell me your goals and your budget. I'll tell you what I would check first, then scope a paid media Sprint or an AI Clarity Session if there's work to do.

    • Foundations Sprint, paid media, 30 days$3,500
    • AI Clarity Session, one week$1,500

  2. Build

    I bring you the strategy and the scope. You approve them. Then I rebuild the campaigns and tracking, or test the workflow against the way your team works.

  3. Run

    We set a date and work backward from it. If ongoing help makes sense, I stay in the account or keep the system working as your process changes.
