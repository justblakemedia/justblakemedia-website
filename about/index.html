Founder + operator

Built by someone who still runs the work.

I run the paid media and build the AI systems myself. You are not hiring a team that manages your account, you are hiring me. That is the whole pitch, and it is also the constraint: I take on a small number of clients and say no to the rest early.

The short version

Six years at Cox. One year going deep on AI. Then I stopped looking for the next seat.

I led paid social at Cox Enterprises for six years, running dozens of campaigns across dozens of accounts at once, then planned digital investment for agency clients as a senior media manager at 22squared. Every account I have picked up since needed the tracking fixed before it needed strategy, and every one had a pile of busywork between the tools that nobody was automating.

So the business is those two things. The senior operator who fixes the measurement first, and the person who builds the automation around the account so it runs without the owner grinding. Most clients start with one and end up buying both.

Full time on Just Blake Media since September 2025. Marietta, Georgia. No employees, on purpose.

Who I am wrong for

It is cheaper for both of us to find this out now.

  • You only run Google search. Meta and LinkedIn are the core, and I do not sell search as a standalone service.
  • You want one person doing content and paid media together. That is two jobs, and the second one suffers.
  • You need someone in your office every week. I am in the account every week, which is a different thing.
  • You spend under $5,000 a month on ads. A retainer is the wrong shape; ask about Amplify instead.

How I work

Five things you can hold me to.

  • Tracking before budget

    No budget moves until the measurement is trusted. Every account, every time.

  • Created paused

    Anything that spends, sends or publishes waits for a person. The system proposes with the dollar impact. You confirm.

  • A number and the reason

    Reports say what happened and why, in a form you can forward. Not a dashboard screenshot.

  • Reconciled to your data

    The platform's count gets checked against your own sales or lease data before anyone trusts it. When they disagree, that is the finding.

  • No on the first call

    If I am the wrong fit, you hear it on the first call instead of the third, and you leave with a name who is right.
