Facebook and Instagram. The core. Local services, real estate, consumer brands, nonprofits with a donor base. The platform where a $1,000 month can move a rental portfolio, and where most accounts I open are running on a boosted post and a pixel that fires on the wrong page.

What I do: campaign structure built around one outcome, audiences tested against each other rather than stacked, several creatives running so the data picks the winner, budgets that follow results with a pacing alert before an overspend.
