Amplify · self-serve paid social

A media buyer in your pocket.

You already shot the photo, wrote the caption, and posted it after closing. It reached the same people it always reaches. Amplify takes the posts you already made and turns them into a small campaign for people nearby, with a budget you set and a plan you can read before it runs. Nothing spends until you switch it on.

Who it is for

For the business that posts to its own page and has never opened Ads Manager

Amplify is for

Small budgets, real posts

  • Local businesses posting to their own Facebook or Instagram page
  • Creators who want a post seen past the followers it already reaches
  • Anyone looking after pages for several businesses at once
  • Ad budgets under $5,000 a month, where a retainer is the wrong shape
  • Getting seen by people nearby: traffic and awareness

Amplify is not

What it will not pretend to be

  • A content service. It amplifies what you already made, it does not make it
  • A promise of leads or sales. If conversions are the job, that is managed paid media
  • A boost button. Several posts, an audience chosen on purpose, a plan you can read
  • Autonomous. Every campaign is created paused, and you are the one who switches it on

How it works

Six taps from your page to a campaign you can read

  1. Connect your Facebook page

    Sign in with the account that already runs your page. Amplify reads your recent posts and nothing else.

  2. Pick the posts and set the budget

    Choose which posts deserve a wider audience. Set the monthly amount. The budget decides how many posts can run at once, and it never goes over.

  3. Read the plan, then switch it on

    Who sees it, what they see, what it costs, on one screen in plain language. A real campaign is built in your own ad account, paused. It runs when you say so, and stops when you say so.

Behind the screen it is a real Meta campaign, ad set and up to five ads, created in your own ad account and owned by you. The demo above runs every screen on sample data: it is not connected to Meta, does not check billing, and does not create anything.

Where it stands

In beta, run with a person watching

The wizard is built: connect a page, pick the posts, set a budget, and a paused campaign lands in your own ad account. Billing is the last piece still being finished, so for now I set each business up myself and watch the first campaign with you. If managed paid media is the better fit, I will say so.
