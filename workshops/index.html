Workshops and coaching

You do not need to learn AI. You need to pick the one thing that ate your week.

Live, in your tools, on your real examples. More time working than watching slides. Everyone leaves with one task handed off and a plan for the next one, not a list of tools.

Two subjects

Same shapes, two subjects. Pick the one your team is losing hours to.

  • Subject one

    AI for marketing teams

    Writing and editing in your own voice, turning one piece of content into five, brainstorming headlines, reading a metrics file, donor-centric or customer-centric storytelling. A few minutes on prompt structure, then the rest of the time working. Everything runs on the free tier of whichever assistant your organisation already uses.

    What you leave with: one task on rung one, a job description you wrote, and a slop check for everything the tool writes next.

  • Subject two

    Paid social fundamentals

    Boosting versus ads versus a campaign. What an objective is and why the wrong one wastes the budget. Audiences, creative testing, the pixel and what it should fire on, and how to read a report so the number cannot be argued with. Built for owners, creatives, and marketers who inherited an ad account.

    What you leave with: an account structure drawn for your business, and the three questions to ask before any money moves.

Formats

Four ways to run it.

Prices depend on the room and are quoted on request. Every session for a company ends with the same slide: the paid first step for that practice, so the workshop is the top of the funnel and not the funnel.

  • One-to-one coaching

    Ninety minutes, screen share, your account. Sold in prepaid packs, and only after a Clarity Session or Sprint, because coaching without context is unpaid consulting with a receipt.

  • Single session for a company

    Sixty to ninety minutes, live, customised to your stack and your examples. Twenty people is the sweet spot; thirty works with table groups.

  • Two-session series

    Session one teaches the method and starts the build. Homework in between. Session two finishes the build on real inputs and sets the next task. The shape that has produced the most change.

  • AI operations working day

    A full day, onsite or remote, building alongside your team. Best after a Clarity Session has already named the tasks.
