WHERE IT STARTED

Before this, a listing hit AppFolio and waited.

Tallahassee, Florida is home to Florida State University and FAMU. It's a market where quality rentals lease fast, especially in the summer turnover window when thousands of students and young professionals are actively searching. In this environment, visibility isn't just helpful. It's the whole game.

The property manager had built a solid portfolio of 42 properties spread across Tallahassee, ranging from one-bedroom apartments at $995/month to five-bedroom homes at $2,950. They knew how to manage properties. They had no idea how to market them. Listings went on AppFolio. They waited. Sometimes units sat vacant for two, three weeks before the right person found them.

The math on vacancy is brutal. A $1,500/month unit sitting empty for three weeks means $1,125 in lost rent before you factor in mortgage, taxes, and insurance. Multiply that across a 42-property portfolio, and the cost of slow promotion compounds quickly. They needed every listing in front of the right people the moment it became available. They needed a system.

Before I Started

  • Zero paid ads, ever
  • Listings promoted through AppFolio and Zillow only
  • Average time-to-lease: 2-3+ weeks
  • No way to promote 42 properties simultaneously
  • No automation, every action was manual
  • No data on which listings were slow, or why
THE CHALLENGE

This wasn't a campaign problem. It was a workflow problem.

Running 42 individual property ads manually isn't a strategy. It's a full-time job nobody signed up for.

Problem 01: Scale

42 listings to promote. One person to do it.

Manual ad creation for each listing, writing copy, uploading photos, setting targeting, going live, takes 30-45 minutes per property. For 42 properties in constant rotation, that's not a task. That's a career.

Problem 02: Timing

In Tallahassee, good listings lease in days.

The Tallahassee rental market moves fast. Near FSU and FAMU, quality units get multiple applications within 72 hours of going live. A listing that isn't actively promoted in that window loses applicants to whoever is advertising. Slow promotion equals lost tenants.

Problem 03: Wasted Spend

Ads running after a unit leases = money burned.

Without automation, it's easy to miss the moment a lease is signed. An ad running for a unit that's no longer available is a common, expensive, and completely avoidable problem.

THE SOLUTION

An ad machine that runs without you.

From new listing to live ad in hours. From signed lease to paused ad in 24 hours. No manual steps.

Step 01

New listing added to AppFolio

Step 02

Step 03

Step 04

Renters see the listing, click through to apply

Step 05

Unit leases, ad pauses automatically

Step 06

Price drops, creative updates automatically

Step 07

Dashboard tracks all 42 ads in real time

Campaign Structure

CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization), Traffic objective, Tallahassee geographic targeting. A single campaign managing all listings simultaneously, with budget optimizing toward whichever properties are generating the most engagement.

Creative Automation

Each ad built from listing data: address, beds, baths, square footage, rent, availability date. No manual copywriting. No manual photo uploads. The system does it, every time, for every listing.

Real-Time Monitoring

A live dashboard shows every ad's status (active, paused, archived), spend, CTR, and lease status. When a unit leases, it appears in the dashboard within 24 hours and the ad pauses automatically.
