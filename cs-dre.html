WHERE IT STARTED

The listings were there. Paid promotion was missing.

This Tallahassee property manager published rentals in AppFolio. They had not run paid ads for the portfolio. I built Meta ads for the listings and managed the changes that came with them.

Each listing needed the right photos, rent and availability before an ad could go out. A new listing meant a new build. A price change or signed lease meant finding and updating the right ad.

The work is detailed, but the problem is simple: an available unit needs promotion and a leased unit should stop spending. I handle both in the client's ad account.

Before I Started

  • Zero paid ads, ever
  • Listings promoted through AppFolio and Zillow only
  • No paid-media baseline for lease timing
  • No repeatable Meta ad build for new listings
  • No paid-ad process for price changes or signed leases
  • No Meta results to compare with the lease log
THE CHALLENGE

Every listing needed an ad and someone to keep it current.

The account needed a repeatable way to build, approve, update and pause listing ads.

Problem 01: Scale

42 listings to promote. One person to do it.

Each listing needs its own details and creative checked before the ad is built. I work through those steps in the client's account.

Problem 02: Timing

A new listing needs someone to act on it.

I build the ad when the listing and media arrive, then send it for review. The listing does not go live because a bot noticed it in AppFolio.

Problem 03: Wasted Spend

Leased units should stop spending.

When the client confirms a lease, I pause the ad. Price changes get checked and updated in the existing ad.

THE SOLUTION

I build the ads. The client approves what runs.

This is an active, hands-on workflow in the client's Meta account. New ads start paused and go live after approval.

Step 01

New listing added to AppFolio

Step 02

I build a paused Meta ad from the listing and client media

Step 03

The client reviews the preview; the approved ad goes live

Step 04

Renters see the listing, click through to apply

Step 05

The client confirms a lease; I pause the ad

Step 06

I update price changes in the existing ad and check the result

Step 07

I compare ad activity with the listing and lease log

Campaign Structure

CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization), Traffic objective, Tallahassee geographic targeting. A single campaign managing all listings simultaneously, with budget optimizing toward whichever properties are generating the most engagement.

Listing ad builds

I use the listing details and client media to build each ad. The ad starts paused. The client sees a preview before it runs.

Updates and checks

I check the ad against the listing when a price changes or a lease is confirmed. The status change is an active step, not an automatic feed from AppFolio.
